Long-term themes (5–10 years)

AI compute and the power bottleneck

Hyperscaler capex is about $600-700B in 2026 and heading toward $1T. US utilities plan about $1.3-1.4T of capex through 2030. Data centers are projected to reach about 9% of US electricity by 2030, and grid interconnection takes 5-7 years.
Beneficiaries: Electrical equipment (transformers, switchgear), grid-scale utilities with data center load, gas turbines, cooling and thermal management, data center REITs, networking optics

Nuclear renaissance

Federal loan support ($17.5B conditional for 10 AP1000s), Big Tech power purchase agreements (more than 10 GW), and global SMR programs.
Beneficiaries: Uranium miners and enrichers, reactor and component suppliers (AP1000 supply chain), nuclear-heavy independent power producers

Western rearmament and drones

NATO 5% of GDP framework by 2035, two active wars, and stockpile replenishment.
Beneficiaries: Defense primes, munitions, counter-drone and electronic warfare, space and satellite, aerospace forgings and castings

Electrification and critical minerals security

Copper is near records on structural deficits. China's rare-earth leverage is unresolved through the Jan. 10, 2027 truce, and Washington is funding non-China supply.
Beneficiaries: Copper miners, rare-earth and magnet producers, domestic lithium and graphite processors

Reshoring of pharma and advanced manufacturing

Section 232 pharma tariffs and MFN deals tie tariff relief to US plant commitments. Chip tariffs and export controls push supply chains onshore.
Beneficiaries: CDMOs, engineering and construction firms, industrial automation, US-based pharma API makers

Aging population and health care demand

Health care is the only consistent source of job growth (+17k in September), and Medicare Advantage ratings and policy remain key drivers (Humana +12% on 2027 stars).
Beneficiaries: Managed care with Medicare Advantage scale, medical devices, obesity/GLP-1 franchises, home health

Cybersecurity

Geopolitical cyber conflict and AI-enabled threats make spending non-discretionary through cycles.
Beneficiaries: Platform security, identity, cloud and endpoint security vendors
